Storm Safety Actions

Taking the right actions before, during, and after a storm can help protect individuals, families, and communities. Learn how to prepare and stay safe during severe weather.

BEFORE A STORM
Protect Your Home

Hover to see more

Protect Your Home

Secure outdoor furniture and loose objects so they cannot be blown away or damage your home. Trim trees that could fall onto your house, and keep drains, gutters, and downspouts clean. Check your roof, windows, and doors for possible water leaks.

BEFORE A STORM
Prepare Emergency Supplies

Hover to see more

Prepare for Emergencies

Sign up for local emergency alerts and monitor weather reports and news. Prepare a backup battery for your phone and a battery-powered radio. Pack a disaster kit, learn first aid and CPR, and practice emergency plans with your family.

DURING A STORM
Stay Updated with Weather Alerts

Hover to see more

Stay Updated with Weather Alerts

Pay attention to weather alerts and local information. Follow updates about the storm and understand emergency warnings. Postpone outdoor activities when thunderstorms are forecast.

DURING A STORM
Find a Safe Shelter

Hover to see more

Find a Safe Shelter

When you hear thunder, go inside a sturdy building. Move to a basement or a small interior room on the lowest floor, away from windows and glass doors. Avoid using electrical devices connected to outlets and avoid running water during the storm.

DURING A STORM
Avoid Dangerous Areas

Hover to see more

Avoid Dangerous Areas

Stay away from trees, sheds, and open outdoor areas during thunderstorms. If flooding occurs, move to higher ground. Never walk, swim, or drive through floodwater. Continue moving toward a sturdy shelter if you are outside.

AFTER A STORM
Check Safety and Get Help

Hover to see more

Check Safety After the Storm

Stay away from fallen power lines, poles, and wires, and report them to the utility company. During power outages, use flashlights instead of candles. Keep generators and grills outdoors, away from windows, and throw away food that has become wet or warm.

Important Safety Reminder

Always follow official weather warnings and instructions from local authorities. Do not return to dangerous areas until they are declared safe.
